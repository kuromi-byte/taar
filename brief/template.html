4. Transparent Methodological Boundaries & Limitations

  • Modeled, Not Measured: Signal power is mathematically estimated using standard propagation formulations and 30m Copernicus/SRTM DEM elevation data. It is a screening triage tool and does not represent physical RF drive-test measurements.
  • Unvalidated for Karnataka: Published studies in other regions report propagation model errors of roughly 5 to 11 dB. The model has not been empirically calibrated for Hassan district. The ±6 dB Uncertain band reflects this range explicitly.
  • OpenCellID Baseline as Lower Bound: Existing tower evidence is sourced from OpenCellID crowd-sourced logs, which are biased toward highways and larger settlements. Missing tower records are shown as Unknown, never claimed as confirmed dead zones.
  • Survey Shortlist, Not Siting Decision: Land ownership, 3-phase electrical grid reliability, and fiber rights-of-way cannot be determined from open geospatial data. Output is a triage shortlist to prioritize field teams, not a final civil engineering decision.
